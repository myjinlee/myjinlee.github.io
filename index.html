Myungjin Lee

I am an MS/PhD student in the Multimodal AI Lab at Ewha Womans University. My research focuses on multimodal large language models, with a particular interest in audio-visual long video understanding and efficient token compression for omnimodal models.

My recent work explores training-free token compression for omnimodal large language models. I aim to reduce the computational cost of long-form video understanding while preserving task-relevant visual, audio, and textual information.

My broader research interests include multimodal large language models, audio-visual understanding, efficient inference, and generative models.
